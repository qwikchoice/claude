# Resume Customization Guide — Divakar BV

## Philosophy
A customized resume is not a different resume — it's the same resume with the right things
turned up and the wrong things turned down. The base content stays true to Divakar's actual
experience. What changes is emphasis, ordering, and language mirroring.

## ATS Optimization Rules

### Keyword mirroring
- Use the JD's exact terminology wherever natural. If the JD says "cross-functional alignment",
  use that phrase — don't paraphrase it as "team coordination".
- Scan JD for role-specific terms: product area, methodology, tools, domain. Each should appear
  at least once in the customized resume.
- Common TPM/PM keywords to watch for and insert naturally:
  - "roadmap", "OKRs", "KPIs", "stakeholder alignment", "executive reporting"
  - "Agile", "Scrum", "sprint planning", "backlog prioritization"
  - "go-to-market", "launch", "cross-functional", "data-driven"
  - Domain terms: "AI/ML", "search", "infrastructure", "platform", "marketplace"

### What ATS systems flag negatively
- Tables, columns, text boxes (break parsing — Divakar's ATS-friendly base avoids these ✅)
- Images or graphics in the resume body
- Non-standard section headings (use: Summary, Experience, Skills, Education, Certifications)
- Fancy fonts or symbols

## Section-by-Section Rewriting Guide

### Profile Summary (top of resume)
This is the highest-impact section — ATS and humans both read it first.

**Formula**: [Role title matching JD] + [#] years + [top domain from JD] + [2 biggest outcomes]

**Example for a Meta-type AI PM role:**
> "Senior Technical Product Manager with 15+ years of experience in AI/ML product leadership
> at Meta and Amazon. Defined and shipped AI-driven search and recommendation features at global
> scale, delivering $60M+ in revenue. Expert in cross-functional roadmap execution, executive
> alignment, and data-driven product strategy."

**Example for an AWS-type TPM role:**
> "Senior Technical Program Manager with 15+ years leading cloud infrastructure and platform
> programs at Amazon and Meta. Owned EC2 region launches, international rollouts across 16 countries,
> and Prime Day risk management for mission-critical search services."

Always end the summary with 2-3 skill tags that mirror the JD's must-haves.

### Experience Section — Bullet Rewriting

**Prioritization per role:**
- Keep 3-5 bullets per relevant role; compress or drop bullets for unrelated work
- Lead bullets should match JD's primary requirements
- Use the STAR-lite format: [Action verb] + [what] + [result/scale]

**High-value bullets from Divakar's background (deploy based on JD focus):**

For AI/ML PM roles:
- "Defined and executed 3-year AI/ML product roadmap for FB Marketplace Search, implementing
  dynamic filters, free items module, and recommendation features — exceeded all topline engagement KPIs"
- "Delivered $60M in search revenue via AI/ML annotation and relevance projects across 15+ countries"
- "Spearheaded RAG LLM application strategy at SynergIQ; guided NVIDIA Conference 2024 presentations"

For Cloud/Infrastructure TPM roles:
- "Managed product roadmap for 10 AWS EC2 regions; authored pricing proposals securing CEO approval"
- "Standardized and automated processes across international, product, technical, and finance teams at AWS"
- "Led Prime Day 2019 risk mitigation plan covering 6 Amazon Search services across multiple teams"

For International/Scale Program Manager roles:
- "Launched Alexa NFSI in 6 international markets (UK, Canada, India, Australia, Japan, Germany)"
- "Managed annotation and audit workflows between vendors and partners across 16 countries"
- "Built cross-functional team synergies across 7 teams to meet i18n milestones"

For Leadership/Executive Reporting roles:
- "Owned senior leadership reporting for Amazon Search CX across 16 countries — key metrics, trends, CX/revenue"
- "Authored pricing proposals and feature forecasts for Director, VP, and CEO review at AWS"
- "Managed delivery of 60 weekly and 45 critical releases across 18 Mastercard workstreams"

### Skills Section Ordering

Order clusters based on JD emphasis:

**If AI/ML focus:** Lead with AI/ML cluster → then Product/Program → then Engineering → then Data
**If TPM/Infrastructure focus:** Lead with Cloud/Infra → then Program Management → then AI/ML → then Engineering
**If PM/Strategy focus:** Lead with Product & Program → then Business Strategy → then AI/ML → then Engineering

### What NOT to Change
- Company names, dates, titles — these are factual and ATS-verified
- Education section — leave as-is
- Certifications — leave as-is (always relevant)
- Metrics and numbers — only use what's real; never inflate

## Change Log Format

After customizing, always output a change summary:

```
CHANGES MADE:
1. [Profile Summary] → Rewrote to emphasize "AI infrastructure" and "platform PM" → Matches JD requirement: "5+ years AI platform experience"
2. [Meta bullet 1] → Changed "improved engagement" to "exceeded MLI and UX KPIs" → Mirrors JD language: "define and track KPIs"
3. [Skills order] → Moved AI/ML cluster to top → JD headline requirement is Generative AI experience
4. [Amazon Search bullet 2] → Added "cross-functional alignment across 7 teams" → JD requires "stakeholder management"

ATS KEYWORD CHECK:
✅ Present: AI/ML, roadmap, cross-functional, executive reporting, data-driven, Agile
⚠️ Added: OKRs, stakeholder alignment, platform
❌ Not naturally insertable: [list any JD keywords that couldn't be added without fabricating]
```

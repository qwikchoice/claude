# Cover Letter — Recruiter Outreach Template Guide

## Purpose
A recruiter outreach letter is a professional cold contact. The recruiter is busy and gets
many messages. Your goal: stand out in 10 seconds, make your value obvious, and get a reply.

## Tone
- Confident and direct — no fluff
- Keyword-aware — include role-relevant terms that match the job description
- Value-first — lead with what you bring, not what you want
- Specific — show you've done homework on the company

## Structure (3 paragraphs, ~200-250 words)

### Para 1 — The Hook (~60 words)
Open with your most impressive credential + a specific outcome. Don't start with "I am writing to..."
Start with a bold claim that's immediately credible:

**Good example:**
"I led AI/ML product roadmaps at Meta and Amazon — delivering $60M in search revenue
across 15+ countries and launching Alexa in 6 international markets. I'm now exploring
Senior TPM/PM roles in AI and would love to connect about [Role] at [Company]."

### Para 2 — Role Alignment (~100 words)
Show you've done homework. Connect 2-3 of your specific experiences directly to:
- The company's known products, challenges, or strategic direction
- The role's key responsibilities (if you have the JD)
- Why you're a strong fit for their stage/domain

Use specific examples from Divakar's profile:
- AI/ML at scale (15+ countries, FAANG brands)
- Technical depth + executive communication (authored CEO-level pricing proposals at AWS)
- Cross-functional leadership (7+ teams, C-suite reporting)
- Current AI work (Agentic AI, RAG LLM at SynergIQ)

### Para 3 — CTA (~40 words)
Close with a specific, low-friction ask:
"I'd welcome a 15-minute call to explore whether there's a fit. My resume is attached —
happy to answer any questions or share more context."

## ATS Keywords to Include (when relevant)
For TPM roles: Technical Program Manager, Cross-functional leadership, OKRs, Roadmap, Agile, Stakeholder management
For PM roles: Product roadmap, Go-to-market, Customer insights, Prioritization, Data-driven, A/B testing
For AI roles: AI/ML, Generative AI, LLM, Recommendation engine, Search relevance, Prompt engineering

## What NOT to do
- Don't start with "My name is..." or "I am writing to express my interest..."
- Don't list every job — save that for the resume
- Don't be vague ("I have many years of experience in technology")
- Don't forget a CTA — every letter must end with a specific ask
- Don't exceed 250 words
